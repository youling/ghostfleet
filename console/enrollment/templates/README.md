# 纳管模板 / Enrollment Templates

## 中文

纳管时选择和编辑 desired management posture 的产品面。模板表达 capability/provider options 与 policy intent，不保存 secret plaintext。具体部署的“完全纳管”等默认组合属于 deployment overlay。

## English

Product surface for selecting and editing desired management posture during enrollment. Templates express capability/provider options and policy intent, never secret plaintext. Deployment-specific defaults such as a "fully managed" mode remain overlays.
